PIR structure comes from 
Xray structure of the HIV-1 Integrase (IN) CCD and CTD in complex with PIR
PBDID 8A1Q

Parameters are derived from analogy via CGenFF and lone pairs are commented to avoid compatibility ussues with CHARMM lonepairs in NAMD.
If lone pairs are necessary they can be uncommented from the str file and the lone pair atom charge subtracted from the CLA atom.
